Stack layers

Training samples

Draw labelled polygons and points for each class on the map, with ready-made land-cover class palettes, or import a shapefile, GeoJSON or KML.

Raster → table

Turn any image (multispectral, hyperspectral, SAR, embeddings) into a table: one row per pixel, a column per band, with optional coordinates and the ground-truth class, ready for Classical ML (tabular data).

Make training data

Cut large images and their ground truth (class raster or shapefile) into matching image / label patches for deep learning: patch size and overlap in metres, an area of interest, classes.txt and dataset.json.

Embeddings menu

Satellite embeddings and small models

AI embeddings sum up a whole year of satellite data in every 10 m pixel. Download them free, explore them, and train very small models on them.

Download embeddings

Google AlphaEarth Foundations (64 dimensions, CC BY 4.0) and TESSERA from the University of Cambridge (128 dimensions, CC0), 10 m, 2017–2025, for any area, without an account. See which years exist before downloading; get only your area, as one GeoTIFF with every band, plus a colour view.

Explore embeddings

Click a place and every pixel is scored by how alike it is (cosine similarity): find all fields, lakes or buildings like the one you clicked. Make a colour view, or classify the layer quickly.

Convert embeddings

Change how an embedding is stored: 8-bit (AlphaEarth coding, or scaled per band) ↔ 16 / 32-bit float. Small files to keep, floats to train on, with how much the values change.

Train embedding model

Train a light segmentation model on an embedding layer (all 64 / 128 bands) and your labelled polygons, points or a class raster such as ESA WorldCover: 256 × 256 patches, flips and rotations, class weights, early stopping and a report, then the class map. These models have 0.17–0.94 million parameters, so they train in minutes, even on a laptop.

Air & rain

Tools ▸ Interpolation and the Forecast menu

From readings at points to maps, and from the past to the next hours, days or months.

Interpolation

A continuous surface (GeoTIFF) from values at points (air-quality stations, rain gauges, soil samples, wells), on the local UTM grid, cut to any boundary, with a leave-one-out comparison of the methods (RMSE, MAE, bias). Kriging also gives its standard error.

IDW (inverse distance weighting)Kriging (ordinary)Spline (thin-plate)Natural neighbourNearest neighbourTrend surfaceTIN (triangulated irregular network)

Get AQI & weather data

Hourly PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, SO₂, CO, O₃, dust and the Indian AQI (computed the CPCB way), and temperature, humidity, dew point, rain, cloud, pressure, wind and sunshine, for up to 500 points: up to 92 past days plus the next 1–16 days of weather forecast. Free, from Open-Meteo (CAMS and ECMWF).

Train forecasting model

Any table with a date column, one series or thousands (stations, grid points), hourly to monthly. One model learns from all series: past values and averages, the same time in earlier cycles, hour / weekday / season, nearby stations, latitude / longitude, and other inputs such as weather (future values taken from a forecast when the table has them). Step-by-step or direct forecasting (automatic picks the better), backtests spread over the last year against two baselines, an 80 % uncertainty band, what the model relies on, a forecast table and points on the map.

LightGBMXGBoostGradient boosting (scikit-learn)Random ForestLinear (ridge) regressionSame time last cycle (baseline)Last value (baseline)

Forecast with a model

Run a saved forecasting model on newer data (e.g. today's readings and the weather forecast): the next steps for every series, with the band, as a table, a chart and a map.

Data, maps & workspace

File, Library, History and View menus

The GIS around the tools.

Add data

GeoTIFF, JPG / PNG with world files, Sentinel .SAFE folders or zips, Shapefile, GeoJSON, KML / KMZ, CSV, Excel and Parquet. Tables with latitude / longitude columns become points on the map.

Data library

Ready-made GIS data from Hugging Face, free: 307 layers of India (states, districts, sub-districts, villages, constituencies, city wards, highways, railways); search and add, each file downloads once.

Tables & editing

Tables and attribute tables under the map: sort, filter, a field calculator (area, perimeter), right-click menus on columns and cells (delete, rename, statistics), pandas scripts, and save-or-discard editing.

Map tools

Click any pixel to see all its bands; right-click the map to copy coordinates (also UTM) and see what's there; band combinations (colour infrared, SWIR, agriculture, urban…), layer metadata and per-band statistics; streets, satellite and topographic basemaps; light and dark themes.

History

Every tool run with its settings, outputs and time: open the results again, Run again, or Change settings & run.

Projects, export & jobs

Keep layers, tables, models and results in one project folder that autosaves; export any layer as GeoTIFF, PNG, Shapefile, GeoJSON, KML, CSV or Parquet; every long run is a background job you can follow, read the log of, or cancel.
