From a satellite image to a land-cover map

Find a place, download a cloud-free Sentinel-2 composite and ESA WorldCover labels for it, and classify the image with Random Forest. The honest score on held-out pixels is 56.6 % (kappa 0.49): WorldCover is itself a 10 m model map, so the labels are imperfect, and the report says which classes get confused.

  1. Tools ▸ Find imagery: search “Hebbal Lake, Bengaluru”, a 5 × 5 km area
  2. Download ▸ cloud-free composite (median of the 6 clearest dates, January–March 2026, 10 m)
  3. Download ▸ land-cover labels (ESA WorldCover 2021)
  4. Tools ▸ Classical ML for raster: image + labels, Random Forest
  5. The map, a confidence layer, the confusion matrix and feature importance

Satellite embeddings and a tiny model

Google AlphaEarth embeddings (64 numbers per 10 m pixel) for 6 × 6 km of north Bengaluru: click a lake to find every place like it, then train TinyUNet, a 0.24-million-parameter segmentation model, on ESA WorldCover labels.

  1. + Add data: the AlphaEarth 2021 GeoTIFF (64 bands)
  2. Embeddings ▸ Explore: click a lake ▸ Find similar places
  3. Embeddings ▸ Train embedding model: TinyUNet, 128 × 128 patches
  4. 70.9 % accuracy, mIoU 43.8 %, kappa 0.62 in about a minute on a MacBook

Count vehicles in an aerial image

YOLO26 trained on the DOTA aerial dataset finds 678 objects (570 small vehicles, 95 large ones, 12 ships) in a 0.6 m NAIP image of downtown Seattle, in 3.4 seconds, with rotated boxes.

  2. Tools ▸ Detect object ▸ YOLO26 aerial (DOTA)

  4. A vector layer of outlines with class, score and size

Forecast tomorrow's air quality, and map it

Delhi's 44 monitoring stations: 30 days of hourly AQI and weather, a forecasting model compared against simple guesses (here the simple guess wins, and the app says so), and tomorrow's AQI kriged over the city's 273 wards.

  1. + Add data: a CSV of the stations becomes points
  2. Insert ▸ Library ▸ Delhi city wards
  3. Forecast ▸ Get AQI & weather data (30 days + 7-day weather forecast)
  4. Forecast ▸ Train forecasting model, 24 hours ahead
  5. Put on the map ▸ Make a map: kriging in CPCB AQI colours
